Hi, I'm Sharon.

Open any website and I'll start reading it to you on my own. Tap the mic and just talk — say stop to stop me, pause or resume to control the voice, or tell me what to focus on. Tap the mic to mute the microphone, or the speaker button beside it to mute my voice.

Settings

When this is on, Sharon reads each page to you on her own. When it's off, she stays quiet until you ask her to read or ask a question about the page.

Let Sharon scroll the page for me.

With this on, you can say “scroll down”, “scroll up”, “go to the top” or “bottom”, or “read more”, and Sharon scrolls the page and reads what's now in view — handy for reading further down a long article or message thread. When it's off, she never touches the page and just reads what's already on screen.

Let Sharon act on the page (click & type).

When this is on, you can ask Sharon to do things on the page — like “reply and say I'll be there”, “search for blue shoes”, or “open the first result” — and she'll click, type, and scroll to carry it out, one step at a time. This is powerful, so it's off by default. She never enters passwords or payment details. Say “stop” at any time to halt her.

When on (recommended), Sharon tells you what she's about to do and waits for you to say “yes” before clicking or typing. Turn it off to let her act without asking each time.
